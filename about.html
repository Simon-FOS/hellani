Our Story

From humble beginnings to becoming Abuja's trusted source for premium food products

Welcome to Hellani's Store

Hellani's Store is a premium food processing company dedicated to providing natural, nutritious, and delicious food products to health-conscious individuals and families across Abuja and beyond.

Founded with a passion for healthy living and authentic Nigerian cuisine, we take pride in our carefully selected ingredients and meticulous processing methods that preserve the natural goodness of every product.

Our journey began in the heart of Kubwa, Abuja, with a simple mission: to make high-quality, natural food products accessible to everyone while promoting healthy eating habits and supporting local agriculture.

Our Journey

2015 - The Beginning

Hellani's Store started as a small family business in Kubwa, Abuja, focusing on providing fresh, locally sourced food products to the community.

2017 - Expansion

We expanded our product line to include processed foods like powdered beans, tigernut powder, and various flours, responding to customer demands for convenience without compromising quality.

2019 - Quality Recognition

Our commitment to quality earned us a loyal customer base and recognition as one of Abuja's most trusted suppliers of natural food products.

2021 - Digital Transformation

We launched our online store to make our products more accessible to customers across Abuja and neighboring states, with reliable delivery services.

2023 - Present Day

Today, Hellani's Store offers over 30 premium products, serving thousands of satisfied customers with our commitment to quality, natural ingredients, and excellent service.
