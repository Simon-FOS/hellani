Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery areas and charges?

We deliver across Abuja and surrounding areas. Delivery within Kubwa is ₦500, while other areas in Abuja are ₦1,500. Delivery times are typically 1-3 hours for orders placed before 3 PM.

Do you offer bulk discounts for large orders?

Yes, we offer special pricing for bulk orders and wholesale customers. Please contact us directly for bulk pricing and customized orders.

How do I place an order?

You can place orders through our website by adding products to your cart and checking out via WhatsApp, or you can call us directly at +234 806 699 2355 to place your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfers, and payment through various digital platforms. Please confirm available payment options when placing your order.

Are your products 100% natural?

Yes, all our products are 100% natural with no artificial additives, preservatives, or chemicals. We take pride in offering pure, wholesome food products.

Can I visit your store to purchase directly?

Absolutely! We welcome customers to visit our store at Zakibiam street, behind New Life Assembly Redeem Church phase 3 Kubwa, Abuja. Our friendly staff will be happy to assist you.
